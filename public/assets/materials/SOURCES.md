# Material and image sources

- Concrete PBR maps: https://polyhaven.com/a/chipped_concrete
- Moss shoots and texture atlas: https://polyhaven.com/a/moss_01

Downloaded through the official Poly Haven asset API; source files remain local. The hero preserves the exact monogram as opaque beige concrete, with small irregular cursor-local moss and triplanar mapping.

Creative reference board: https://in.pinterest.com/19s1750/intake-logo/
Read as art direction: soft moss cushions, delicate plants, exposed roots and concrete/nature contrast.

`moss-carpet.png`: native ImageGen, 2026-10-19. Seamless top-down macro moss albedo with deep forest green/olive cushion rosettes, fine filaments and occasional brown peat under diffuse lighting. Used with low-amplitude bump; scanned moss shoots remain instanced above it.

`../butterfly.png`: existing native ImageGen photographic butterfly asset, reused for the hero. Hinged wings and an orbit around the concrete monogram supply real depth.

- cast-concrete-photo.png: native built-in ImageGen reference-derived photographic concrete tile, based on user images (30).jpeg. Replaces procedural speckles; triplanar colour and photograph-derived relief.
